How it works

Three steps. Zero busywork.

No accounts, no setup, no shopping for the right plan. Open the canvas, write a problem, and the tutor starts watching.

01

Show the problem

Write by hand on the whiteboard, type, snap a photo of your textbook, or paste a screenshot. Markov reads ink, print, and pictures with equal fluency.

02

Work through it

The tutor watches every stroke. After each pause it reads what you have, checks your steps, and asks one guiding question. Never the final answer.

03

Keep the reasoning

Markov ends every session with the why, not the result. Parents see a digest of what was learned. Teachers see where each student got stuck.

FAQ

Common questions.

How is this different from ChatGPT or Khanmigo?

Markov.ink lives on a whiteboard and reads what you write by hand. Chat tutors cannot see the page. We can.

Does the tutor just give answers?

No. The Strict Socratic mode refuses to reveal the final answer until you have shown a step. Pedagogy first.

What subjects does it cover?

Math from algebra through calculus, chemistry, physics, English essay outlining, and more. Anything you can write on a board.

Do I need an Anthropic API key to try it?

The prototype runs with your own key for live AI. Six scripted demos work without a key for first time exploration.

Where does my data go?

Your API key stays in your browser. Canvas content is sent only to Anthropic's API for the live read. No third party trackers.

Is there a mobile app?

The web app works on iPad and Android tablets today. Native apps with stylus palm rejection ship within six months.

How do schools buy it?

Three month design partner pilots first, then per student annual licensing. Email us, we will set up a call.

Why I am building this

I trained as a mathematician, taught myself programming during the pandemic, and watched a generation of students learn to ask ChatGPT for the answer instead of thinking through the problem. The shortcut to a wrong understanding is faster than ever, and the cost is a generation that knows how to type but not how to reason.

Markov.ink exists because the tutor I wanted at 17 did not exist. It watches you work. It asks the next question. It refuses to do the math for you. The pedagogy comes from Plato through Pólya, and the math behind the AI is Markov, the man who proved that the next state depends on the current one. The current state is your handwriting. The next step is the question you should ask yourself.
